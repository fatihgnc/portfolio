No sign-up, no username, no feed. Sign in with Apple is the only door, and it
never asks for a name or an e-mail.

Coordinates are rounded to 5 km on the device before anything is sent, so the
exact point never leaves the phone. A secret carries a coarse place name and
nothing else.

Written in a week, in App Store review right now.
